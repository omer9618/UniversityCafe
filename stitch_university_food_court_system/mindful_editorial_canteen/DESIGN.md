## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines the frantic campus food hall as a calm, considered culinary ritual. Blending the tactile warmth of natural stoneware with the structural clarity of an independent print periodical, it counters digital exhaustion with grounded, rhythmic restraint. The emotional tone is deliberate, warm, and restorative—trading aggressive sales nudges and visual clutter for quiet confidence, generous breathing room, and artisanal utility.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Editorial Minimalism with Warm Tactile Grounding**:
- **Print Editorial Sensibility:** Lowercase serif typography establishes an unhurried, humanistic voice, contrasted against precise, monospaced ledger details that evoke kitchen weigh-ins, ingredient purity, and transparent sourcing.
- **Organic Earth Palettes:** Natural pigments replace synthetic digital tints. Cream paper bases, unglazed clay accents, and deep charcoal typographic ink create visual comfort during extended browsing between classes.
- **Architectural Framing:** Subtle hairline rules (1px) establish structural containment without visual weight. Cards float as framed botanical or culinary plates, prioritizing studio-level ingredient visibility and macro textures over hyper-saturated staging.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from raw earthenware, sun-dried parchment, and carbon ink. High saturation and synthetic brights are strictly avoided to ensure a sustained, low-cognitive-load ordering experience.

- **Primary (`#C2593F`):** Deep terracotta/rust red. Used sparingly for critical focal points: active states, primary purchase triggers, focused borders, subtle brand badges, and accented dietary markers.
- **Secondary (`#E5DDD5`):** Warm clay outline. Applied to structural container borders, horizontal divider rules, and secondary badge perimeters to separate surfaces with subtle clarity.
- **Tertiary (`#F4EFEB`):** Muted sand/warm beige surface. Serves as the primary container background for menu item cards, drawer interiors, and selectable modules against the canvas.
- **Neutral (`#1F1E1D` / `#2B2927`):** Deep charcoal ink. Replaces harsh true black for all primary display headings, body narratives, and functional prices, maintaining organic softness against the parchment canvas.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm off-white/light cream backdrop that anchors the entire UI in a tactile, paper-like atmosphere.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between editorial elegance and ledger-like culinary bookkeeping:

- **Headline Hierarchy (`Newsreader`):** Display headings and food dish titles use italicized or normal lowercase treatments (e.g., *“daily, fresh, campus fuel”* and *“slow-simmered dal makhani”*). This stylistic choice removes corporate coldness and conveys artisanal craft.
- **Body Text (`Manrope`):** Descriptions of flavor profiles, preparation techniques, and dietary warnings rely on a neutral, open sans-serif to preserve high legibility across mobile touchscreens.
- **Labels, Metrics, & System Data (`Space Mono`):** Prices, gram weights, macronutrient stats, preparation counters, and categorical classification tags (e.g., `[ BIRYANI ]`, `₹180`, `450g`) are rendered in tracked monospaced caps to evoke vintage kitchen tickets and inventory sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a structured yet airy editorial fluid grid designed for rapid scanning and relaxed contemplation:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays employ a 12-column layout with 24px gutters and generous 48px outer margins. Handheld viewports collapse to a 4-column framework with 16px gutters and 20px outer page margins.
- **Editorial Proportions:** Food cards and presentation panels prioritize tall vertical aspect ratios (3:4 or 4:5), ensuring dish imagery sits gracefully above detailed ingredient copy rather than competing horizontally.
- **Breathing Negative Space:** Spacing scales favor larger outer margins and gaps (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) between distinct categories, avoiding dense e-commerce stacking in favor of intentional pause points.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating digital dropshadows, blurred glass, and extruded physical surfaces, maintaining a flat, print-like sheet structure:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Hairlines:** Depth is primarily communicated through crisp, 1px perimeter borders (`#E5DDD5` base, shifting to `#C2593F` when active or hovered).
- **Tonal Layering:** Visual hierarchy relies on stacked planes of raw pigment: the `#FAF8F5` canvas base supports `#F4EFEB` card trays, which house crisp `#FAF8F5` internal inset badges.
- **Tactile Press States:** When interactive elements (e.g., dish selection cards, add-to-tray levers) are activated, elevation does not raise toward the user via shadows; instead, borders thicken to 1.5px and backdrops gently warm from `#F4EFEB` to `#EFE6DF`.

## Shapes

Corner radii balance architectural precision with soft, warm hand-feel:

- **Container Modules:** Standard ordering cards, summary sidebars, and dialogue sheets utilize consistent medium radii (16px / `rounded-lg`) to soften structured 1px framing lines.
- **Top-Center Pill Insets:** Categorical classification tags pinned at the top-center border of item cards employ full pill shapes (`rounded-full`), visually anchoring each card like an archival tag.
- **Interactive Levers:** Quantity toggles and inline operational buttons use modest 8px rounded corners to maintain mechanical, utilitarian utility.

## Components

### Card Containers
- **Anatomy:** Vertical 3:4 orientation with `#F4EFEB` surface fill, encased in a 1px continuous border (`#E5DDD5`).
- **Pinned Category Tag:** Centered at the top edge, overlapping the top border line: a pill chip (`#FAF8F5` background, 1px `#E5DDD5` stroke) featuring tracked `label-sm` monospace text (e.g., `[ BIRYANI ]`, `[ BOWL ]`).
- **Presentation:** High-key, natural light studio photography of dishes placed on unglazed earthenware, cropped cleanly with internal padding of `space-md` around content blocks.

### Buttons & Order Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Deep terracotta (`#C2593F`) background with off-white (`#FAF8F5`) text. Set in `label-md` uppercase monospace with slight tracking. No shadow, 8px corner radius.
- **Secondary / Ghost Actions:** `#FAF8F5` surface with 1px `#E5DDD5` perimeter border and `#1F1E1D` text. Upon hover, the border shifts to `#C2593F`.

### Chips & Dietary Markers
- **System Tags:** Monospaced, enclosed in brackets (`[ VEG ]`, `[ JAIN ]`, `[ KITCHEN CLOSING: 14:00 ]`).
- **Style:** Transparent background with subtle 1px border. Dietary indicators use tiny 6px geometric dot fills (deep terracotta for house signatures, earthy olive for plant-based).

### Input Fields & Search
- **Structure:** Clean underline or fully framed 1px container with muted sand (`#F4EFEB`) fill. Text renders in `body-md` charcoal ink.
- **Placeholder:** Styled in lowercase `Newsreader` italic for a warm, literary invitation (e.g., *“search by grain, spice, or kitchen...”*).

### Dietary & Macro Badges
- **Ledger Insets:** Secondary nutrition cards displaying protein, caloric value, and allergen statements feature strict table-grid hairline divides (`#E5DDD5`), styled as a culinary receipt or apothecary label.
